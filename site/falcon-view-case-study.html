S Problem statement · Lean UX canvas

Hospitals ran on lagging, siloed data, and no one could act in time.

Hospitals burn 2.5× the energy and about 133,000 gallons of water a day compared with a typical commercial building. The cost and compliance pressure were real, but the deeper failure was informational.

S Context · sizing the world the system lives in

Five pressures hit at once, and not one was visible in time.

Secondary research and our own site study found five compounding pressures. Pick a domain for the evidence. The failure underneath all five is the same.

S The friction, quantified

Seven systems and a phone tree stood between a reading and a decision.

S Ecosystem · the system sits in an orbit

Fifteen actors, three orbits, one question: who acts this morning?

The platform couldn't serve all fifteen equally. Distance from the daily decision set design priority. The inner orbit acts every morning; the outer orbits set the rules and pay the bills.

S Cognitive load · what a supervisor held in their head

Everything a manager needed lived in six places and one memory.

A Research · methods, synthesis, the person it was for

147 observations pointed to eight recurring themes.

Layered deliberately: benchmark the macro landscape, then talk to people, and let the synthesis, not the brief, set the structure.

A The pivotal insight

Motivation wasn't the bottleneck. Visibility was.

Six of eight themes described friction; two described ambition held back by it. The job wasn't to motivate change. It was to remove the friction blocking it.

LEARNING 01

The reframe was the real deliverable

Moving from "sustainability dashboard" to "the one view four roles act on this morning" reorganised everything downstream.

NEXT TIME

Hold the KPI ceiling from day one

I let stakeholder requests push the first canvas to 12 KPIs; testing forced the cut to ≤7 anyway. I'd defend the ceiling from the start.

LEARNING 02

Low-power users can be the real audience

Maintenance crews scored low power, high stake, and were nearly treated as recipients. Promoting them shaped the alert-and-work-order flow.

tap · next time ↻
NEXT TIME

Bring maintenance in earlier

Their position surfaced late and reshaped the build. That insight was cheap to get and expensive to get late.

tap · back ↻
LEARNING 03

Augment beats replace

The clean-slate rebuild is the instinct; the evidence said the opposite. Wrapping the trusted legacy system is why adoption held.

tap · next time ↻
NEXT TIME

Make security a week-1 stream

IT's SSO and IoT concerns nearly stalled rollout. Next time, security research runs from the first week, not the last.
